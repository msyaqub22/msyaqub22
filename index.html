About

I graduated in Electrical Engineering from Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute (GIKI) in 2026. My degree took me through embedded firmware on STM32 and Nordic chips, communication systems from AM radio to visible-light data links, and a final-year project on a portable fetal heart rate monitor built around a multi-transducer ultrasound Doppler array.

Today I work as an AI/ML Developer at Raythorne, building and keeping reliable a full-stack hiring platform where a conversational voice agent runs sales interviews end to end. I enjoy the debugging side of engineering most: finding the race condition, the timing fault or the noisy signal that makes a system misbehave, and fixing it properly.
